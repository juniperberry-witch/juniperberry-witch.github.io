## Description
Three Sisters Center's Hollerbier Haven: Volume 3 Issue 4. "Farming has always been an essential and defining characteristic of the Pennsylvania Deitsch way of life. In the time that the Deitsch have been on Pennsylvania soil, however, the face of farming has changed dramatically. The Deitsch lived in a way that was close to the land and understood that what is good for the land was good for the farmer and ultimately good for all the people of a region. There has been a disconnect. Modern mechanized farming is not on the side of the land or the farmer or the community, it is on the side of the dollar."
