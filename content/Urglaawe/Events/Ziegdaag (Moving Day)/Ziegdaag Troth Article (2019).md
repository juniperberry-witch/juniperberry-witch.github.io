## Description
All of living existence is on an evolutionary trajectory. The gods created us, but our consciousness is still at a youthful level. Without change, we will not grow. Without growth, we will not be, at the end of this cosmic cycle, where the gods were at the beginning of it. Since everything around us is also changing and evolving, the need for the expansion of our understanding of existence and how the multiverse works becomes even greater. These agents of change make us look at ourselves from many perspectives, some not so flattering.
